Grilled over charcoal,
the moment you order

TAWA is an eel restaurant right outside City Hall Station Exit 10, Seoul, specializing in unagi rice bowls and Nagoya-style hitsumabushi.

Great eel depends
on the fire, and
how it is grilled

Good eel matters, but at TAWA we started with the fire. We tracked down a charcoal brazier handmade by a craftsman in Japan, and every eel is grilled over that charcoal only after you order.

Crisp and savory outside,
moist inside.

You taste the eel itself together with a gentle smoke from the fire. We don't cut corners on ingredients, and we care about every step: the charcoal, the brazier and the grilling.

Hitsumabushi:
one bowl, three ways

Hitsumabushi is a Nagoya specialty of grilled eel over rice, and our signature dish. Here is how to enjoy it.

  1. 一

    As it is

    Start with the eel and rice just as they come.

  2. 二

    With garnishes

    Next, add nori, wasabi and the other garnishes.

  3. 三

    As ochazuke

    Finally, pour in the warm broth and finish it as ochazuke.

Right outside City Hall Station (Seoul Subway Lines 1 & 2) Exit 10, on basement level 1 of the Hansan Building, next to the Korean Air building. An easy walk from Seoul City Hall and Deoksugung Palace.

  • From the main road (Exit 10 side), turn right just after Exit 10 and go straight; the entrance is on your left.
