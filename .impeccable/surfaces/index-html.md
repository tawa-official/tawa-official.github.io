# 타와 홈 (index.html)

Scope: the whole one-page homepage. Mode: Persuade (visitors decide whether to come; action = 네이버 예약 / 전화 / 길찾기).

Audience & job: 시청 office workers (lunch, 회식) and 덕수궁 weekend visitors, mostly on phones from Naver/Instagram; they need what 타와 is, the signature, prices, hours, how to get there, then book on Naver.
Proof on hand: owner photos (spreads, charcoal grilling, interior, rooms, entrance), the owner's 18 s reel, full menu with prices, owner's story text. Hero image chosen by owner: the set-table spread.

History: first build used the 木札 tag-wall direction (seed:14a2c9759735bacf, position 7). Owner rejected it as 촌스럽다 and pinned "고급 프리미엄" — a user-pinned direction, which beats the roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a jubako (重箱) seen from above — black lacquer outside, the food glowing inside; photography carries everything and the interface recedes to hairlines. It refuses the brush-lettered, wood-textured "Japanese restaurant" kit and the gold-on-black luxury cliché.

OWN-WORLD: Lacquer black #121010 ground; warm off-white #ece5d8 text; the menu printed on washi #f1efeb exactly as the store's own menu book; one lacquer-interior vermilion #b03a26 used only for 추천 marks, the quote rule and the "오늘" hours flag. Hahmlet light for headlines, Noto Sans KR for reading, Shippori Mincho for the Japanese sub-labels and TAWA wordmark. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows, no gradients except the legibility scrim on the hero photo.

STORY: Visitor sees the full table, reads that eel is grilled to order over a Japanese charcoal brazier, learns the three ways to eat hitsumabushi, scans the menu with prices and photos, sees the rooms and corkage, gets hours/parking/directions, books on Naver or calls.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed spread photo; transparent header with TAWA 田和 wordmark, nav and a hairline 예약 button; bottom-left headline "주문이 들어오면, 숯불에 굽습니다" at display scale, one-line lede, solid off-white 네이버로 예약하기 + ghost phone button, live Seoul-time open/closed status beside them.

FORM: user-pinned "고급 프리미엄" direction translated through the store's own jubako lacquer (candidate 6 of the original ordered list). seed:14a2c9759735bacf (degraded roll; superseded by the user pin). Signature interaction: the hero table settles from a slight zoom as the headline rises; header turns to lacquer once the photo leaves; the menu's sticky category rail tracks the section in view; a phone action bar appears after the hero.
