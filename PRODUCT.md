## Product Purpose

The official homepage of 타와 (TAWA), a 장어덮밥 · 나고야식 히츠마부시 specialist near 시청역. Its job is to inform: who 타와 is and why its eel tastes the way it does (the story), what it serves and for how much (menu with photos), and the practical facts (location, hours, parking, corkage, rooms). Success means a visitor leaves knowing what 타와 is and how to come, and those ready to book are handed to Naver.

## Positioning

Eel grilled to order over charcoal in a craftsman-made charcoal brazier (숯화로) sourced from Japan. The owner's belief: good eel matters, but how and over what fire it is grilled makes the bigger difference — crisp and savory outside, moist inside, with a gentle smoke aroma. The signature is Nagoya-style hitsumabushi, eaten three ways in one bowl.

## Product Principles

1. The fire is the story: every page should make "grilled to order over charcoal" felt, not just stated.
2. Inform first: what 타와 is, the signature dish, where and when — within the first scroll on a phone.
3. Naver is the booking door: one clear "네이버 예약" path, never a competing in-site flow.
4. Real food, real words: use the owner's photos and the owner's sentences; mark anything unconfirmed.
5. Serve both scenes: quick weekday lunch near 시청 and an unhurried evening with sashimi and drinks, private rooms included.
